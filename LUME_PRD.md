# LUMÉ — Premium Aesthetic Clinic Landing Page Template

## 1. Product Overview

**Project Type:** Premium interactive landing page template  
**Brand Concept:** LUMÉ  
**Industry:** Aesthetic Medicine / Beauty / Dermatology  
**Market Reference:** Riyadh, Saudi Arabia  
**Primary Purpose:** Portfolio showcase + reusable client template

LUMÉ is a fictional premium aesthetic medicine brand used to demonstrate a high-end web design and development capability. It is **not** intended to operate as a real clinic system.

The goal is to create a visually exceptional, conversion-focused landing page that can later be adapted for different clinics by changing branding, content, imagery, doctors, treatments, and contact information.

## 2. Product Vision

Create a website that makes a visitor feel the quality of a premium aesthetic clinic before they ever visit the clinic.

The experience should feel:

- Premium
- Editorial
- Modern
- Clinical but not sterile
- Elegant
- Confident
- Technology-forward

The site must not feel like a generic medical template.

## 3. Primary Objective

The main objective is **conversion-oriented brand presentation**.

The experience should:

1. Capture attention immediately.
2. Establish a premium brand identity.
3. Make treatments easy and attractive to explore.
4. Build trust through doctors and results.
5. Keep the booking CTA visible and obvious.
6. Demonstrate strong creative frontend skills to potential clients.

## 4. Target Audience

### Primary

Adults approximately 25–45 in Riyadh and other Saudi markets who are interested in aesthetic medicine, skincare, rejuvenation, injectables, or beauty treatments and expect a premium digital experience.

### Secondary

Potential clinic owners, medical groups, beauty brands, and agencies evaluating the developer's design and frontend capabilities.

## 5. Core User Journey

```text
DISCOVER
    ↓
DESIRE
    ↓
EXPLORE
    ↓
TRUST
    ↓
CONSIDER
    ↓
BOOK
```

The website should continuously guide the visitor toward **Book Consultation**.

## 6. Landing Page Structure

### 6.1 Navbar

Requirements:

- Minimal premium navigation
- LUMÉ logo/wordmark
- Navigation anchors
- Arabic / English switch
- Persistent booking CTA
- Mobile menu

Primary CTA:

**BOOK CONSULTATION**

### 6.2 Hero

Core concept:

> **YOUR SKIN.**  
> **REIMAGINED.**

Supporting narrative:

> **SKIN → SCIENCE → RESULTS**

Requirements:

- Strong editorial typography
- Cinematic imagery or video
- Initial entrance animation
- Clear primary CTA
- Secondary exploration CTA
- Premium art direction

The hero is the most important visual section.

### 6.3 Brand Narrative

Introduce the philosophy of the brand using:

**SKIN → SCIENCE → RESULTS**

This section should use scroll-based storytelling rather than a conventional text block.

### 6.4 Treatment Explorer

Allow visitors to browse based on concerns rather than only medical terminology.

Example concerns:

- Glow
- Hydration
- Acne
- Pigmentation
- Fine Lines
- Contouring
- Rejuvenation

Interaction can reveal recommended treatments dynamically.

### 6.5 Featured Treatments

Show a curated selection of premium treatments.

Each treatment should include:

- Name
- Short description
- Visual
- Category
- Optional metadata such as duration/recovery
- Explore CTA

Focus on visual storytelling, not dense medical information.

### 6.6 Before / After

Create a high-quality comparison experience using a draggable before/after slider.

Each result may include:

- Treatment
- Doctor
- Sessions
- Timeframe

Include a suitable disclaimer for fictional/demo content where necessary.

### 6.7 Doctors

Present doctors as premium editorial profiles rather than traditional CV cards.

Each profile can include:

- Portrait
- Name
- Specialty
- Experience
- Areas of expertise
- Languages
- Booking CTA

### 6.8 Interactive Treatment Journey

Example:

```text
01  CONSULTATION
       ↓
02  PERSONALIZED PLAN
       ↓
03  TREATMENT
       ↓
04  AFTERCARE
       ↓
05  RESULTS
```

Use scroll-driven transitions and imagery to communicate the experience.

### 6.9 Packages

Present packages as premium treatment programs rather than discount cards.

Example:

**THE LUMÉ GLOW**

- Personalized consultation
- Skin analysis
- Multiple treatments
- Aftercare program

CTA:

**DISCOVER PACKAGE**

### 6.10 Trust / Testimonials

Optional section for:

- Patient testimonials
- Brand statistics
- Certifications
- Trust signals

Content should remain visually restrained.

### 6.11 Final CTA

Large closing statement with a strong conversion moment.

Example:

> **READY TO REIMAGINE YOUR SKIN?**

Primary CTA:

**BOOK CONSULTATION**

Secondary:

**CHAT ON WHATSAPP**

### 6.12 Footer

Include:

- Brand information
- Contact details
- Riyadh location
- WhatsApp
- Social links
- Language switch
- Navigation
- Disclaimer/copyright

## 7. Conversion Strategy

The conversion action must remain visible throughout the page.

### Desktop

Persistent booking CTA in the navbar.

### Mobile

Sticky bottom booking action.

### Secondary Conversion

WhatsApp should always remain easy to access.

Contextual WhatsApp messages may be generated from the section being viewed.

## 8. Arabic / English

The template must support both:

- English LTR
- Arabic RTL

Arabic should be treated as a first-class visual layout, not simply translated text.

The design system must account for:

- RTL navigation
- Typography
- Spacing
- Alignment
- Motion direction
- Component layout

## 9. Creative Direction

### Visual Keywords

- Luxury
- Editorial
- Minimal
- Organic
- Scientific
- Soft technology
- Premium hospitality

### Avoid

- Generic hospital aesthetics
- Heavy blue medical UI
- Cheap gradients
- Generic stock imagery
- Dashboard-like cards
- Excessive rounded containers
- Overly flashy animation

## 10. Typography Direction

Use a strong display typeface paired with a clean UI/body typeface.

The typography system should create a contrast between:

```text
EDITORIAL HEADLINES
        +
MODERN UI
```

Arabic typography should visually complement the Latin system.

## 11. Motion Direction

Motion is a major part of the product experience.

Use motion for:

- Hero entrance
- Typography reveals
- Image reveals
- Scroll storytelling
- Hover transitions
- Before/after interaction
- Treatment explorer
- CTA micro-interactions
- Page transitions where useful

Motion should feel expensive and controlled, not decorative for its own sake.

## 12. 3D Direction

3D is an optional visual enhancement.

Possible use cases:

- Abstract organic forms
- Liquid/glass objects
- Floating sculptural elements
- Subtle particles

3D must support the brand rather than dominate the website.

## 13. Demo Functionality

This is a **demo/template**, not a production clinic system.

### Required mock functionality

- Treatment exploration
- Before/after slider
- Language switching
- Booking form interaction
- Booking confirmation state
- WhatsApp CTA
- Navigation interactions

### Not required

- Real authentication
- Real patient accounts
- Real appointment availability
- Real payment processing
- Real medical records
- Real CRM
- Real clinic administration
- Full CMS
- Complex backend

## 14. Reusability Requirement

The most important architectural/product goal is to make the project reusable for future clients.

The content and branding should be separated from the visual components.

Reusable configuration should cover:

- Brand name
- Logo
- Colors
- Typography
- Contact details
- Social links
- Hero content
- Treatments
- Doctors
- Packages
- Results
- Testimonials

A new clinic should be achievable primarily by changing configuration, content, and visual assets instead of rebuilding components.

## 15. User Stories

### Visitor

As a visitor, I want to understand the brand immediately so I know whether it feels relevant and premium.

### Treatment Discovery

As a visitor, I want to explore treatments by concern so I can discover suitable options without knowing medical terminology.

### Trust

As a visitor, I want to see doctors and realistic treatment results so I can build confidence in the clinic.

### Conversion

As a visitor, I want to book a consultation without unnecessary friction.

### WhatsApp

As a visitor, I want to contact the clinic through WhatsApp when I have questions before booking.

### Arabic

As an Arabic-speaking visitor, I want the Arabic experience to feel native and polished.

### Template Owner

As a developer, I want to replace the fictional clinic's branding and content quickly so I can reuse the template for another client.

## 16. Success Metrics

Because this is a showcase template, success should be evaluated through both product and portfolio criteria.

### Experience Metrics

- Strong visual impact within the first screen
- Clear booking CTA
- Smooth mobile experience
- High-quality motion and micro-interactions
- Fast perceived performance
- Consistent bilingual experience

### Portfolio Metrics

- Can be demonstrated in under 60 seconds
- Looks custom rather than templated
- Shows advanced frontend capability
- Demonstrates responsive design
- Demonstrates interaction design
- Easily adaptable to another clinic

These should only be considered when adapting the template for a real client:

- Real booking backend
- Calendar integrations
- CMS
- Admin dashboard
- CRM integration
- WhatsApp Business API
- Email/SMS notifications

## 19. Product Principle

> **Make the visitor feel the premium experience before they ever enter the clinic.**

The website should not feel like:

> "Here are our services."

It should feel like:

> **"This is where I want to be treated."**
